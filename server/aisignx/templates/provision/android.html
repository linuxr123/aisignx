Set up an Android tablet as a kiosk

For a tablet or phone that has been factory reset and is on its first welcome screen. It downloads the AiSignX app from this server, becomes a managed device (Device Owner), pairs itself, and can then be locked down and controlled from the web page. Everything on the device is replaced, so use a device that holds nothing you need.
